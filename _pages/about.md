I am a First-Year PhD candidate at NASP (Universities of Milan and Turin) studying Sociology and Methodology of Social Research.

My academic work draws on Gender Studies and Cultural Studies to examine how popular culture reflects and reproduces social identities. My current doctoral project investigates contemporary representations of masculinity in rap music, analyzing how gender norms intersect within the cultural narratives of the genre.
